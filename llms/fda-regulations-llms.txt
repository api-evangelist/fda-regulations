# FDA Regulations

> The body of federal rules the U.S. Food and Drug Administration enforces over food, human and
> animal drugs, biologics, medical devices, cosmetics and tobacco — codified at 21 CFR — and the
> one machine-readable surface FDA publishes for that regulatory activity: the FDA Data Dashboard
> API (DDAPI), operated by the Office of Inspections and Investigations (formerly the Office of
> Regulatory Affairs).

Generated by API Evangelist on 2026-09-12 from this repository's artifacts and from FDA's own
public documentation. This is a third-party profile; FDA does not publish an llms.txt of its own
(probed 2026-09-12: https://www.fda.gov/llms.txt returned 404).

Read this first if you are an agent: every one of the four operations is a POST, and every one of
them is a READ. The API signals failure inside the JSON body with a numeric `statuscode`, not with
HTTP status — `400` means Success and `401` means Not Authorized. Credentials are two headers,
`Authorization-User` (your approved email) and `Authorization-Key` (FDA-issued), and FDA issues
them free on request. TLS 1.2 is required.

## APIs

- [FDA Data Dashboard API](https://datadashboard.fda.gov/oii/api/index.htm): Four POST search
  endpoints over FDA's compliance and enforcement datasets — inspection classifications,
  inspection citations, compliance actions and import refusals. Base URL
  `https://api-datadashboard.fda.gov/v1`.

## Operations

- `POST /inspections_classifications` (`inspectionsClassifications`): inspection outcomes by firm,
  FEI number, product type, project area and inspection end-date range.
- `POST /inspections_citations` (`inspectionsCitations`): the Act / 21 CFR references cited against
  a firm during an inspection, with short and long descriptions. Carries `ActCFRNumber`.
- `POST /compliance_actions` (`complianceActions`): warning letters, injunctions and seizures by
  firm, product type, FDA center, action type and date taken.
- `POST /import_refusals` (`importRefusals`): shipments refused entry, by firm, country, industry
  and product code, district and refusal date, with the refusal charges cited.

## What is not here

- No MCP server, no A2A agent card, no GraphQL, no AsyncAPI and no webhooks. Probed 2026-09-12;
  FDA publishes none of them for this API.
- No SDKs or client libraries in any registry. FDA's integration guidance is to import ddapi.json
  into a REST client.
- No `/.well-known/` documents on any FDA host, including security.txt.
- No status page, no dated changelog, no deprecation policy, no published rate limits.
